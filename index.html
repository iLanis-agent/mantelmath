Mantel math

A mantel goes wrong at the saw: a shelf flush with the opening and no ears, corbels bunching in the middle, or legs cut before the shelf thickness came off. Mantel math turns the firebox opening into the shelf length with labeled ears, turns the shelf into the corbel count and their centers, and turns the shelf bottom and thickness into the leg height. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Mantel plan - the opening plus labeled ears becomes the shelf length.
  • Corbel plan - the shelf at the labeled span becomes the corbels and their centers.
  • Leg plan - the shelf bottom minus the shelf becomes the leg height.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: 15 cm ears past the opening, a corbel at least every 60 cm of shelf, and a 10 cm shelf thickness - all illustrative defaults you can change by measuring. Real mantels near real fire follow the fireplace maker's clearance-to-combustibles table and the local code. This is layout arithmetic, not a clearance ruling or a safety rating: measure the real fireplace and let the maker's table govern.
